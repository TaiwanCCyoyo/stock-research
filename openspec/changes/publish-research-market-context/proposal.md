## Why

The completed feature-discrimination atlas is usable inside its research branch but is not discoverable from the shared main checkout or verified as a consumer handoff. The retained 0050 benchmark has prices and a coarse descriptor, not a reusable launch/consolidation/decline/resumption dataset.

## What Changes

- Publish the preserved atlas code, contracts and indexes without rerunning or revising its historical results.
- Add a bounded, independently versioned 0050 daily-context dataset with strictly separate past-only descriptors and retrospective labels, using existing pinned local prices.
- Provide small portable evidence packages, explicit bulk-data locators and read-only consumer examples so a new checkout can discover, validate and inspect available artifacts.
- Register actual consumers and remaining limitations; deliver a proposed updated owner goal without activating it.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `research-market-context`: Immutable 0050 context artifacts, provisional definitions, timing separation and read-only access.
- `research-artifact-handoff`: Discoverable, versioned atlas/context handoff with portable summaries and explicit local bulk-data availability.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing experiment acceptance, execution and exposure contracts remain intact.

## Impact

Changes are confined to Stock research modules, scripts/tests, task artifacts, research indexes and documentation. No new dependencies, dashboard page, source download, production cache rewrite, scheduler change, portfolio evaluation, confirmation/holdout access or automatic goal resumption. Market descriptors are research definitions, not calibrated signals or a profitable strategy. Verify synthetic timing/missingness cases, saved-artifact hashes and a downstream read-only smoke check. Docker checks are unnecessary because no Docker execution surface changes.
