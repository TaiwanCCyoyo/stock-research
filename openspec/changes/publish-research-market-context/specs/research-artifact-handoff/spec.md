## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shared discoverability and availability

The research index SHALL point to the atlas and benchmark task reports, contracts, portable manifest and reader. It SHALL identify which files are Git-portable and which require the retained local bulk dataset; file existence SHALL NOT imply strategy approval.

#### Scenario: Fresh checkout without bulk files

- **WHEN** a consumer has tracked files but no original worktree or price cache
- **THEN** portable aggregates and benchmark context remain readable, while bulk access reports its explicit required locator rather than silently rebuilding inputs.

### Requirement: Traceable consumer handoff

The handoff SHALL preserve both unsuccessful and unknown feature comparisons with their denominators and source dataset identity. Consumer status SHALL distinguish a successful data read from an implemented website page.

#### Scenario: Website smoke check

- **WHEN** the website session reads a supplied package successfully
- **THEN** the handoff records the verified dataset identity and scope of that read without claiming UI integration or strategy acceptance.
