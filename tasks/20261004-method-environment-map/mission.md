in bounded batches by industry/role, individual strength and market state, before

## Before the first evaluated child batch

Select a justified small subset, not the Cartesian product of every method,
industry, scale and threshold. Record exact definitions/versions and changes from
prior work; include all tested alternatives and retries in the comparison history.
Pin a development window with its reason and excluded available years, review
exposure, and name the data/universe/adjustment/availability identities actually used.

Fix the opportunity outcome, future horizon and reference point before evaluation.
An ex-post trough-to-peak winner is not automatically an opportunity available at
every earlier stock-date. Define the sampling/episode matching protocol for both
directions of comparison. Preserve ordinary/failed/not-triggered cases, unknowns
and right-censored follow-up. Prevent repeated days, methods, scales or overlapping
industry memberships from becoming independent successes. A later stock rise is
not a captured account hit.

Record counts and uncertainty for winner coverage and selected-candidate success
and non-success; compare the eligible same-context base rate. Keep descriptive
retrospective grouping separate from a predictive PIT experiment. For scored
methods, fix bins/calibration and report support; a score is not a probability.
Historical period/industry stability and incremental value beyond a plain
momentum/context control matter before full-account development.

Freeze each child's allowed variants, comparison count, stop/promotion rules,
runtime budget, required outputs and exact single-run authorization. Use existing
verified tools; document concrete gaps instead of demanding every possible field.
Full manual-resilience and final owner loss-budget rules belong to their later
stage, not a requirement to finish a generic platform before feature discovery.
