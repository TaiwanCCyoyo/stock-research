# Publication deadline acceptance matrix (design only)

The [data contract](data-contract.md) defines the candidate-content protocol;
the [handoff](implementation-handoff.md) records outstanding semantic review.
Every row below is a **required future synthetic assertion, not a test result**.
O2b-A runs documentation checks only. No v3 implementation or real-root probe
exists in this change. Retain PR102's original failed publication-deadline evidence.

Times below use Asia/Taipei on `D = v1.freeze_deadline(day)`'s date. `B` means
the whole covered bundle (day, cohort proposal, manifest); `R` means a complete
valid durable acceptance receipt. `J` is start/terminal/ledger. “Accept” means
content acceptance under the proposed protocol, not proof of receipt readability
before D. Recovery always requires verified released local ownership; otherwise
it stays pending. All injected clocks, file roots and child processes are fixtures
owned by the future test.

Assertions must inspect file presence, exact identities/bytes, reader decisions,
attempt state and cohort anchor at the indicated checkpoint. A reproduction of
legacy false success is evidence of the defect, not a passing repair. Hashes and
mtime alone cannot prove a deadline. Recovery does not claim hardware/power-loss
durability or automatic exactly-once repair at every crash point.
