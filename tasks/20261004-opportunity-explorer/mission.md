# Opportunity explorer and shared comparison interface

Registered 2026-10-04 before implementation. The owner requests completion of
Claude's accepted presentation prototype and coordination with the sector-wave
and K-line research chats. This is an additive application/data-adapter task,
not approval to run a new strategy, unseal results, download or rebuild the
full historical inventory. Existing studies and their verdicts remain unchanged.

## Accepted presentation choices

Use launch <= observation date < end confirmation for main-map membership;
keep unlaunched waves in the directory and an explicit omitted count. Retain
consolidation within the wave. Missing prices and snapshot censoring are not
exit events. Area uses gain since one explicit representative wave start,
equivalent diameter proportional to gain, with one fixed cross-date scale.
Reliable historical cap may multiply area by 1.25 per 100x cap. Zero gain has
zero quantitative area; a separate outline marker may remain selectable.

The map, ranked list, holdings ribbons and date controls share one observation.
Industry cells contain stock cells, with rounded organic shapes and gaps.
Forward confirmed exits fade into particles, reverse scrubbing does not burst.
Theme and reduced-motion controls apply throughout. The old lab becomes a
stock-detail drilldown. Do not silently reuse the old rolling-window selection.

## Inputs and metrics

Read existing bounded wave-lab cases and source identities. Keep synthetic
interaction data explicitly separate from historical candidate data. The new
JSON contract is additive and contains sources, coverage, representative
intervals, waves, phases, holdings and allocations. Historical roles require
effective-dated evidence; current classifications remain labelled as such.

Capital exposure uses a same-date observed/reconstructed allocation and its
declared completeness. No equal-weight imputation, snapshot forward filling,
missing-as-zero or reconstructed profit claims. A descriptive positive-price
move/holding-date overlap, if provided, must never be called actual profit.
Final comparison semantics are coordinated with the research chat and documented
before any research evaluation. The UI and CLI must call the same comparison
module. A visualization is not an acceptance gate or a trading signal.

## Delivery and verification

Deliver usable desktop/mobile controls, search/pin/up-to-four comparisons,
capital cards, moving stock ranks, shared-date ribbons, evidence and research
method inspection, plus a CLI reading the same versioned bundle. Verify
geometry area and temporal scale, unknown coverage, partial exits and date
boundaries with meaningful fixtures; test actual browser drag, selections,
themes and small-screen behavior. Preserve unrelated `.claude/launch.json`
and Claude's ignored handoff/prototype files.
