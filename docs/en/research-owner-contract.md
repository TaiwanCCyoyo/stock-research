## Non-hit clarification after resumption — 2026-09-08

The owner confirmed that an **account hit must actually earn the large return**.
A stock rising after it was sold is selection opportunity evidence only, not a hit
that pays for losses or interrupts the account's non-hit sequence. The exact return
threshold and observation/settlement horizon remain to be fixed before evaluation.

The owner did **not** approve letting profits on other holdings offset the failure
budget. Their clarification: repeated non-hits' small wins and losses must not
accumulate into a large loss; a large winner elsewhere must not excuse that loss.

drawdown or an executable misses-only counterfactual. Twenty attempts/about 20% is
an owner warning reference pending calibration, not a newly approved exact veto.
