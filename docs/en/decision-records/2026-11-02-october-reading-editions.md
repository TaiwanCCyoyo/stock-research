# October report reading editions (P7)

Date: 2026-11-02. Implements P7 of the [dashboard readability decision](2026-11-02-dashboard-ux-and-report-readability.md). This batch adds a separate Traditional Chinese reading edition and `report-reading.v1` sidecar for each of the 20 assigned studies. It changes no original report, mission, result, HTML page, receipt, source snapshot, or digest.

## Why this change

Receipt-first narratives, stale preparation sections and later publications obscure what was actually answered. Annual rotation's root v1 is incomplete but its completion-v2 is complete; HHHL v4 has explicit measurement overlays; momentum-only has not answered the incremental-pattern question. A reader should see these boundaries before using a study to select a new direction.

`ready` means the editor found adequate saved text for the bounded reading claim. It does not mean the strategy passed, the owner accepted it, every mission question was completed, or new numerical verification was performed. Parent review, hosted review and integrated browser acceptance remain separate. No assigned complete-text study required `history` solely because its original presentation was difficult. Missing-source and planning tasks and Claude-owned PR113/PR17 reports are excluded.

## Evidence and timing choices

- The nine early October execution/diagnostic tasks have no retained task mission in this checkout. Their questions are reconstructed only from the complete saved reports' stated comparisons; each edition says so. A missing mission is not invented, and a retained result is not mislabeled as missing merely because the mission is absent.
- Eight of these sources state 2019–2023 without an exact starting date. Their editions preserve that year-level window and endpoint context; `data_period` is null instead of inventing January 1. Catalog participation explicitly supplies 2019-01-02 through 2023-12-29. The literature task has no experiment period, so its period is also null.
- Annual rotation's sidecar period is the principal complete holding interval, 2011-01-03 through 2026-01-02. Its 2010 ranking input and separate 2026 YTD ending October 12 are labeled in prose. The completion's 24,710 eligible candidate-stock-years and the v1 summary's broader 25,883 count are not conflated; both preserve 8,413 unknown ranking returns.
- HHHL v4 exposes the four noise-first corrections and the unavailable landmark coordinate overlay. Variants' calendar-full denominator is different from the earlier first-decision summary; the two headline rates are not treated as revisions of one estimate.
- Daily versus sparse observations, retrospective episodes, execution attempts, issuer counts, net profit retention and marked equity are kept separate. Non-hit contributions are not an executable misses-only account, and price paths are not realized account losses.
- Full-history source text includes historical predecessor paths and acquisition limitations. These remain provenance only. This batch reads no predecessor, producer, market input or external data store and does not certify that historical bulk locations are currently available.
- Momentum-only remains a partial answer: pure momentum comparisons are available, pattern decomposition is not evaluated. The November 4 reading revision links the saved pattern-scan stage report, which still describes partial completion. It removes the temporary claim that another session is editing the report; publication of a presentation revision does not complete the missing decomposition or replace this study's saved numbers.
