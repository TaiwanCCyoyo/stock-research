## Unknowns are not an unlimited engineering assignment

For each source gap, record whether it changes eligibility, price/quantity,
spendable cash, attribution or the final verdict. Then choose one explicit path:

1. **Observable and required:** obtain or normalize the necessary evidence,
   preserving raw values and availability dates. Bound the acquisition scope.
2. **Not observable historically:** propose a labelled model with fully rerun
   sensitivities. A stricter fill or later dividend is not guaranteed to reduce
   adaptive-portfolio profit monotonically. Do not claim a conservative bound
   merely because one assumption sounds pessimistic.
3. **Outside the selected strategy's scope:** defer implementation. A regular-lot
   strategy need not wait for historical odd-lot auction reconstruction, but
   changing to regular lots is a new preregistered strategy choice, not silently
   rounding an existing strategy and calling it identical. The final NT$2m/five-
   holding and feasible-execution requirements remain unchanged.
4. **Material to acceptance but unresolved:** the experiment/claim remains
   unavailable. Do not erase missing days, code changes, halted holdings or
   unpaid obligations to manufacture a complete result.

An assumption-supported exploratory result is not proof of real executability.
If the objective cannot be supported without unverifiable historical fills,
report that limit and arrange owner-approved prospective validation rather than
claim success or chase a nonexistent public personal-account receipt field.
