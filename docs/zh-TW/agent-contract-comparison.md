## 研究目的與實際影響

| English                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | 繁體中文                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Calibrate review to the declared research purpose and material impact. Exploratory backtests prioritize direction, fair benchmark comparisons and credible conclusions; complete broker-accounting simulation is required only when that purpose or impact warrants it.                                                                                                       | 依已聲明的研究目的與實際影響審查。探索性回測優先確認方向、比較基準公平及結論可信；只有目的或影響需要時，才要求完整券商帳務模擬。                                                      |
| Correct concrete risks from share-count multipliers, material corporate actions, look-ahead bias or missing data that undermine the result or its interpretation. Security and data-loss risks retain the existing blocking rules.                                                                                                                                            | 股數倍率、重大公司事件、前視偏誤或漏資料若造成破壞結果或解讀的具體風險，仍須修正；安全與資料遺失風險維持既有阻擋規則。                                                                |
| Assess remittance or dividend-credit delays, odd lots and similar details against the study's purpose and plausible impact. A supported low-impact approximation with disclosed assumptions may be a limitation rather than a blocker. Timing near a trade boundary or under cash constraints can change feasible orders and conclusions and must be assessed.                | 匯款或股息入帳延遲、零股等細節，依研究目的及合理影響判斷。有依據的低影響近似且已揭露假設，可列為限制，不自動阻擋；接近交易邊界或資金受限時，日期可能改變可執行訂單及結論，仍須評估。  |
| When impact is uncertain, start with the smallest sufficient sensitivity or impact check, expanding if material uncertainty remains rather than immediately demanding comprehensive implementation. Match evidence to the defect: focused reproduction or direct code evidence can establish an ordinary bug without a full-market run.                                       | 影響不確定時，先做最小充分的敏感度或影響核對；重大不確定性仍在才擴大，不直接要求全面實作。證據須與缺陷相稱；一般程式錯誤可由聚焦重現或直接程式證據確立，不必一律全市場實跑。          |
| For a blocking finding, including a hosted P1/P2, state the concrete trigger, impact, evidence and minimum sufficient correction. Do not raise an issue to P2 merely because the model could be more complete. Keep existing severity, CI, security and merge gates and the final-acceptance requirements in [the owner contract](../en/research-owner-contract.md) in force. | 阻擋 finding（含託管審查 P1/P2）須說明具體觸發條件、影響、證據及最小充分修正；不能只因模型還能更完整就升級 P2。既有嚴重度、CI、安全、合併條件及 owner contract 的最終驗收要求仍有效。 |
